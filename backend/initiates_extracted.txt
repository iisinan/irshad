STATEMENT OF FINANCIAL POSITION REVIEW
The Statement of Financial Position as at half year 2026 reflects a substantial strengthening of the
Company's financial position, with total assets increasing by 276.1% over the prior year to ₦23.40 billion.
This growth was financed primarily through a significant increase in shareholders' equity following capital
injection and strong retained earnings, resulting in a stronger balance sheet and lower financial leverage.
ASSETS
Total Assets
Total assets increased from ₦6.22 billion in YTD 2025 to ₦23.40 billion, representing an increase of ₦17.18
billion (276.1%), reflecting stronger-than-expected business expansion and liquidity.
Non-Current Assets
Non-current assets increased significantly to ₦7.70 billion, compared with ₦2.07 billion in the prior year,
representing a 271.2% increase. The increase was mainly attributable to growth in Property, Plant and
Equipment (PPE) from ₦1.96 billion to ₦4.68 billion (138.4% increase), reflecting continued investment in
operational capacity; recognition of an Intangible Asset of ₦11.0 million; and a substantial increase in
Investment in The Initiates Uganda to ₦3.01 billion, compared with ₦105.6 million in the prior year,
indicating significant strategic investment activities during the period.
Current Assets
Current assets increased from ₦4.15 billion to ₦15.70 billion, representing an increase of 278.6%.
Trade and Other Receivables
Trade and other receivables increased to ₦9.29 billion, compared with ₦3.86 billion in 2025 (140.7%
increase). The age analysis of accounts receivable (₦7.385 billion) shows that 86% of these debts are less
than 60 days old. Major clients were involved in system upgrades resulting in late issuance of supporting
documents for invoicing; we are assured that all outstandings will be paid as soon as possible.
Assets-in-transit — equipment paid for by TIP that is yet to be received, amounting to ₦1.8 billion — was also
included as trade receivables.
Cash and Cash Equivalents
Cash and cash equivalents improved significantly from ₦43.0 million to ₦5.68 billion, representing an
exceptional increase of over 13,000%. This was made possible by the injection of the proceeds from the
hybrid offer. Over 50% of this cash was invested in interest-yielding short-term investments.
Inventories
Inventory increased to ₦94.5 million from ₦59.7 million, reflecting higher inventory holdings to support
increased business activities.
Current Tax Assets
Current tax assets increased substantially to ₦574.2 million, indicating higher recoverable tax balances
compared with the prior year.
EQUITY
Total shareholders' equity increased dramatically from ₦3.31 billion to ₦17.53 billion, representing an
increase of 429.9%. The increase was driven by:
• Share Capital increasing from ₦445.0 million to ₦1.00 billion following additional equity issuance;
• Share Premium increasing significantly from ₦17.8 million to ₦9.56 billion, indicating substantial capital
raised above par value;
• Retained Earnings increasing from ₦2.54 billion to ₦6.66 billion, reflecting strong cumulative profitability;
• Revaluation Reserve remaining unchanged at ₦303.5 million.
The significant growth in equity has considerably strengthened the Company's capital base and financial
flexibility.

The Company's financial position improved considerably during the period, supported by:
• Total assets increasing by 276%;

shareholders' equity and reducing financial leverage through the elimination of long-term debt.

assets position the Company well to support future growth. Going forward, it is the goal of management to
prioritize maintaining efficient working capital management — particularly the collection of trade receivables
— to sustain the Company's strong liquidity and overall financial health.
